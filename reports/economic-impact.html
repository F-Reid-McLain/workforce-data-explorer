The Two Measures
JQI

Job Quality Index

Measures the quality of individual jobs in this sector. Compares average wages to the regional baseline and adjusts for growth — a high JQI means this sector offers above-average jobs that are getting better, not just that the sector is large.

(avg sector wage ÷ regional avg wage) × (1 + growth rate)
ECI

Economic Contribution Index

Measures how much the sector contributes to the local economy as a whole — not the quality of individual jobs. A sector with many workers or a large total wage bill scores high even if individual wages are modest. Adjusted for growth.

(sector wage bill ÷ total local wage bill) × (1 + growth rate)
